# seed_inputs: sources

Everything `make seed` reads is in this folder, so a fresh machine seeds with **no network and no OpenAI key**
Inputs that were not supplied fall back to clearly marked synthetic data (`source =
'synthetic'`, names prefixed `[fallback]` in the database).

**What is real in this build:** only the government documents below (and the OpenFreeMap base map the browser
loads at runtime). **Synthetic:** villages and their boundaries, mandals, roads and water in LandLens's own
layers, development projects and their status histories, master plan zones, parcels, sales, guideline rates,
soil and groundwater. Real OSM inputs (roads, water, village points), hand-digitized projects and the zone legend
are a V1 item.

## Not supplied (synthetic fallback, V1)

| Expected file | Would provide | Fallback used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `osm/*.json` (Overpass export, query in `api/landlens/gen/overpass_query.ql`) | real roads, rail, water bodies, amenities, village points | synthetic villages on a jittered grid, radial highways, a local road grid, tanks, amenities |
| `study_area.geojson` | the confirmed KUDA boundary | a starting box, lat 17.75-18.15 N, lon 79.35-79.85 E |
| `projects/projects.geojson` | hand-digitized geometry and sourced status dates of the 5 projects | approximate synthetic geometry and invented status dates (`api/landlens/gen/projects.py`) |
| `zones/zone_types.json` | zone codes, names and permitted uses from the land-use map legend | an 8-zone placeholder list |

Price levels (`B_INR_PER_SQM` in `api/landlens/gen/params.py`) are placeholders, not calibrated to published
Warangal rates.
